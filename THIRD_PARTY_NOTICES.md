## F1 circuit centerlines

The simplified centerlines for Spa-Francorchamps, Monaco, Suzuka, Monza, Interlagos, and the Hungaroring are derived from [bacinger/f1-circuits](https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits), distributed under the MIT License. The source data describes itself as circuit paths traced from public map references. Antigravity GP simplifies and scales these paths to published lap distances. Elevations, track widths, and presentation are authored by Antigravity GP.

## Bathurst and Charlotte Roval centerlines

The simplified Mount Panorama (Bathurst) and Charlotte Motor Speedway Roval centerlines are derived from [GameDev-Toolkit/global-motorsport-circuits-json](https://github.com/GameDev-Toolkit/global-motorsport-circuits-json), distributed under the Apache License 2.0. The source repository describes its paths as local planar coordinates without a common unit or coordinate reference system. Antigravity GP simplifies and scales these paths to published lap distances. Elevations, widths, and presentation are authored by Antigravity GP.
